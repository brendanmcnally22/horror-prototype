# Halvern Research — material and detail upgrade

Open Halvern_Research_v2.blend. Roof is enabled, and the active camera is the interior corridor. Use rendered or Material Preview mode to see the procedural materials; Solid mode displays only base colors.

792 individually editable parts; 17 materials. Added procedural concrete grain and bump, mottled painted steel, rust, worn linoleum, fabric, wood and paper textures, reflective damp patches, soft edge bevels, floor joints, half-height paint, skirting, room signs, rivets, door hinges, pipe flanges, lamp guards, cabinet vents, medicine bottles, folded blankets, crate slats, and sloped terrain strips. All textures are generated inside Blender; no external texture files are needed.

Interior uses a closed roof with teal fluorescent and red emergency lighting. Preview renders use 8 EEVEE samples for service render limits; increase samples to 64 or higher locally for cleaner results.

Procedural shader textures and Blender modifiers require baking/conversion for equivalent Roblox appearance. The current .blend is the full-fidelity source; the web/GLB preview may show only base colors. Gameplay flicker is still a specification rather than an animation or Roblox script. The previous model and parts schedules are preserved; use parts_v2.json for the updated geometry schedule.
